Privacy Policy (iPhone and iPad)

Ganbaru: Learn Japanese for iOS · Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ganbaru: Learn Japanese app for iPhone and iPad ("the app") handles. The app is published by 4K Anime Wallpapers Dev ("we", "us"). Contact: developerx168@gmail.com. The Android version has its own policy.

In short: an account is optional, we do not ask for your location, the app shows no ads, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Your learning progress (completed lessons, stars, points, level, streak, saved phrases, unlocked topics), your name if you enter one, and your chosen language are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them. Your subscription status is also kept on your device so premium works offline; the information used to check it is described in section 5.

Sign in with Apple and iCloud sync. Signing in is optional; the app works fully without it. If you choose to sign in with Apple, the app receives an identifier for you from Apple and, only if you allow it, your name and email address (or Apple's private relay address). We keep these on your device to show your account in the app and to prefill your name. While you are signed in, the app also copies your learning progress (stars, points, streak, study days, unlocked topics and finished lessons, and your first name or chosen display name) to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can sync between your Apple devices. This copy is stored and protected by Apple under your Apple Account; we do not receive it and have no server that holds it. You can sign out at any time in Profile, and "Delete synced data" removes the iCloud copy. You can also revoke the app's Sign in with Apple access in your Apple Account settings. Apple handles this under its own policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

2. Microphone, speech recognition and voice

Speaking exercises ask for the microphone and speech recognition permissions. When you speak, the app uses Apple's speech recognition. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process your voice on the device or send it to Apple's servers to turn it into text, under Apple's privacy policy. The app itself does not record, save or upload your audio; it only receives the recognized text to check your answer. The microphone is used only while you are speaking in a speaking exercise. You can refuse or withdraw these permissions in Settings and keep using the other exercises.

The app also uses your device's text-to-speech voice to read Japanese phrases aloud. This happens on your device.

3. Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your device at the time you choose. They are created locally; nothing is sent to our servers. You can turn them off in the app or in Settings.

4. Analytics, crash reports and advertising measurement

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. They collect information such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate region, in-app events (for example onboarding step viewed, lesson completed, paywall viewed) and crash logs. This data is not linked to your name or email.

The app shows no ads. To measure our own ads for the app (for example, which ad led to an install or a purchase), the app uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK. When the app first asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, you can choose "Ask App Not to Track" or "Allow". If you allow it, the app sends Meta in-app events (for example first launch, onboarding completed, lesson completed, paywall viewed), your device's advertising identifier and basic device information. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not collected or sent. This is used only for measuring and optimizing our ads. Meta handles it under its own policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy. The app does not use Facebook Login and does not send your name or email to Meta. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card or Apple Account details.

To manage subscriptions and verify purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID that it generates for your installation (it is not your name, email or Apple Account), your purchase and subscription information (which plan you bought, purchase and renewal dates, trial status, price and currency, and whether the subscription is active, cancelled or refunded), your App Store receipt or transaction data, and basic device and app information (such as app version, device model, operating system version, country and language). RevenueCat checks the purchase with Apple and tells the app whether premium is active, so we can unlock premium features and keep them unlocked if you reinstall or change devices and use "Restore purchases". RevenueCat handles this data under its own policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you have allowed tracking (see section 4), the app also gives RevenueCat your device's advertising identifier, your Meta anonymous app ID, your Firebase app-instance ID and your IP address. RevenueCat uses them only to report subscription events (for example a free trial starting, a trial turning into a paid subscription, a purchase or a renewal) to Meta and Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can measure and optimize our ads. These reports contain the event type, price, currency and the identifiers above, but not your name or email. If you decline tracking, the advertising identifier is not sent.

6. Videos

The Video section plays videos from YouTube through YouTube's embedded player. When a video plays, YouTube (Google) may collect data such as your IP address and device information under its own policy at policies.google.com/privacy. If you suggest a video or report a wrong subtitle, we receive the link or the report you send and technical data such as your IP address for abuse protection.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app: Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple, iCloud, speech recognition), Google (Firebase, YouTube), Meta (ad measurement, only if you allow tracking) and RevenueCat (subscription management and purchase verification). We do not sell personal information and do not share it for their own marketing purposes. Those providers act under their own privacy policies.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you uninstall the app. If you used iCloud sync, the iCloud copy stays in your iCloud account until you choose "Delete synced data" in Profile or remove the app's iCloud data in your device settings. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period (analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months by default). RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to run subscriptions and meet its legal and accounting obligations. To ask about or request deletion of data associated with your device, email us at developerx168@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted to the service providers above over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.
