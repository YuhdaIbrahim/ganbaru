Privacy Policy

Ganbaru: Learn Japanese · Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ganbaru: Learn Japanese app ("the app") handles. The app is published by 4K Anime Wallpapers Dev ("we", "us"). Contact: developerx168@gmail.com.

In short: there are no accounts, we do not ask for your name, email or location, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Your learning progress (completed lessons, stars, points, level, streak, saved phrases, unlocked topics), your chosen language and your subscription status are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

2. Microphone and voice

Speaking exercises ask for the microphone permission. When you speak, the app uses Android's built-in speech recognition service. Depending on your phone, this service (usually provided by Google) may process your voice on the device or send it to its servers to turn it into text. The app itself does not record, save or upload your audio; it only receives the recognized text to check your answer. The microphone is used only while you are speaking in a speaking exercise. You can refuse or withdraw the permission in your phone settings and keep using the other exercises.

The app also uses your phone's text-to-speech engine to read Japanese phrases aloud. This happens on your device.

3. Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. They collect information such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate region, in-app events (for example lesson completed, topic opened, paywall viewed, subscription started) and crash logs. This data is not linked to your name or email.

4. Advertising

Free users see ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app first shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform); ads and analytics storage follow your choice, and you can change it any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices when it is offered. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Subscribers do not see ads.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card or account details. We only learn whether a subscription or the lifetime purchase is active, which plan it is and when it changes, so we can unlock premium features.

6. Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app: Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play, speech services). We do not sell personal information and do not share it for their own marketing purposes. Those providers act under their own privacy policies, for example policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period (analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months by default). To ask about or request deletion of data associated with your device, email us at developerx168@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted to the service providers above over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
