Privacy Policy

Ganbaru: Learn Japanese · Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ganbaru: Learn Japanese app ("the app") handles. The app is published by 4K Anime Wallpapers Dev ("we", "us"). Contact: developerx168@gmail.com.

In short: there are no accounts, we do not ask for your name, email or location, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Your learning progress (completed lessons, stars, points, level, streak, saved phrases, unlocked topics) and your chosen language are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Your subscription status is also kept on your device so premium works offline; the information used to check it is described in section 5.

2. Microphone and voice

Speaking exercises ask for the microphone permission. When you speak, the app uses Android's built-in speech recognition service. Depending on your phone, this service (usually provided by Google) may process your voice on the device or send it to its servers to turn it into text. The app itself does not record, save or upload your audio; it only receives the recognized text to check your answer. The microphone is used only while you are speaking in a speaking exercise. You can refuse or withdraw the permission in your phone settings and keep using the other exercises.

The app also uses your phone's text-to-speech engine to read Japanese phrases aloud. This happens on your device.

3. Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. They collect information such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate region, in-app events (for example lesson completed, topic opened, paywall viewed, subscription started) and crash logs. This data is not linked to your name or email.

4. Advertising

Free users see ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app first shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform); ads and analytics storage follow your choice, and you can change it any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices when it is offered. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Subscribers do not see ads.

To measure our own ads for the app (for example, which ad led to an install or a purchase), the app also uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK. Once you have agreed where consent is required, it sends Meta in-app events (for example first launch, onboarding completed, lesson completed, subscription or purchase started), your device's advertising ID and basic device information. This is used only for measuring and optimizing our ads. Meta handles it under its own policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy. The app does not use Facebook Login and does not send your name or email to Meta. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, and in the EEA, the UK and similar regions you can change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card or account details.

To manage subscriptions and verify purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID that it generates for your installation (it is not your name, email or Google account), your purchase and subscription information (which plan you bought, purchase and renewal dates, trial status, price and currency, and whether the subscription is active, cancelled or refunded), your Google Play purchase token, and basic device and app information (such as app version, device model, operating system version, country and language). RevenueCat checks the purchase with Google Play and tells the app whether premium is active, so we can unlock premium features and keep them unlocked if you reinstall or change devices and use "Restore purchases". RevenueCat handles this data under its own policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Where you have agreed to analytics and advertising data (see sections 3 and 4), the app also gives RevenueCat your device's advertising ID, your Meta (Facebook) anonymous app ID, your Firebase app-instance ID and your IP address. RevenueCat uses them only to report subscription events (for example a free trial starting, a trial turning into a paid subscription, a purchase or a renewal) to Meta and Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can measure and optimize our ads. These reports contain the event type, price, currency and the identifiers above, but not your name or email. If you decline, these identifiers are not sent.

6. Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app: Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play, speech services), Meta (ad measurement) and RevenueCat (subscription management and purchase verification). We do not sell personal information and do not share it for their own marketing purposes. Those providers act under their own privacy policies, for example policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period (analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months by default). RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to run subscriptions and meet its legal and accounting obligations. To ask about or request deletion of data associated with your device, email us at developerx168@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted to the service providers above over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
